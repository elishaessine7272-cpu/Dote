What's inside
Built around what you actually do, each day.

Not a spreadsheet with a new coat of paint. Tap any line to see how it actually works.

Ring up sales from a phone or tablet, works even when the network doesn't, and syncs the moment it's back.
Customer orders placed on WhatsApp land straight in your dashboard no copying details into a separate app.
Every sale or booking generates a clean receipt automatically, sent by WhatsApp in one tap.
Stock deducts automatically on every sale. Low-stock alerts before you run out, not after.
See your best and worst days at a glance, with a full history you can page back through month by month.
See your OPay, Moniepoint and bank balances alongside your sales no separate app, no separate login.
Shifts, attendance and pay in one place, with the deductions calculated correctly the first time.
Plain language weekly summaries what sold, what didn't, what's owed to you no spreadsheet required.
