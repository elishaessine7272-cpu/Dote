Your business is one. Your information shouldn’t be scattered

Stock, sales, staff, expenses, and cash flow shouldn't live in separate places. Standpoint brings your entire operation into one connected view, to help you know exactly where your business stands.

Today's reality

This is the honest setup of most growing businesses. It works until money, staff payday, contorl, time start slipping.

Stock

Excel sheet

Type one wrong number, and the count is off with no way to tell who entered it or when.

Sales

WhatsApp orders & Reciept

A payment screenshot sits in a chat thread. It's not a record anywhere else, and once the chat moves on, it's easy to lose.

Expenses

Notebook ledger

Costs are written down as they happen, but nobody checks the notebook until month-end by then, the overspending has already happened.

Payments

POS and bank apps

Sales land in a POS or a bank app, mixed in with personal spending. Knowing exactly what the business has means checking each app by hand.

Cash flow

Head and memory

The owner estimates what the business has instead of checking. The shortage only shows up the day a payment fails.

Staff records

Attendance book

Attendance and most records is written in a book, then copied into payroll by hand. Every copy is a chance for a mistake, which is why staff get paid late even when the money is sitting there.
